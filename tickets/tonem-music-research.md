## Why Not Strictly Classical?

The user requested "классических записей" (classical recordings). However, after extensive
searching through primary sources:

- **Wikimedia Commons:** Virtually all solo classical piano performances are licensed
  CC-BY or CC-BY-SA. Performers like La Pianista (Gnossiennes) use CC-BY-SA 3.0.
  Michael Laucke uses CC-BY-SA 4.0. Kevin MacLeod uses CC-BY. Finding CC0 performances
  of classical compositions is nearly impossible.

- **Musopen:** Their ToS explicitly disclaim responsibility for verifying the license
  status of individual recordings. Most recordings there require CC-BY attribution.

- **YouTube/Internet Archive:** License status is almost always unclear for individual
  recordings.

Therefore, the chosen tracks are **original neoclassical/ambient compositions** that:
1. Are explicitly **CC0** on their source pages
2. Feature calm piano/ambient textures suitable for yoga/relax BGM
3. Are original works (no derivative copyright complications)
4. Are verified through individual page inspection on the upload date

## Recommendations for Future

If strictly classical public-domain recordings are needed in the future, options include:
1. Commission new CC0 recordings from performers
2. Use MIDI renderings of PD compositions (quality varies)
3. Contact performers directly for relicensing
